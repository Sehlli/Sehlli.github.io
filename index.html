About

Designing products that are
functional, manufacturable, and reliable.

I'm a Mechanical Design Engineer with 3 years of experience in product design and development. I specialize in transforming ideas into manufacturable products, from initial concepts and CAD modeling to prototyping, testing, and production-ready designs.

My expertise includes SolidWorks, CATIA, and Fusion 360, with a strong focus on sheet metal design, plastic parts, mechanical assemblies, design for manufacturing (DFM), and engineering documentation. I enjoy solving complex design challenges while balancing performance, aesthetics, cost, and manufacturability.
